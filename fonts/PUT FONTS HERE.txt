Copy your font files into this folder with these exact names
(the HTML file references these filenames directly):

  Poppins-Regular.ttf
  Raleway-Regular.ttf
  Roboto-Regular.ttf
  ink-free.ttf
  NotoMono-Regular.ttf

If any of your files are named differently, either rename them to match
the list above, or open editor.html and edit the @font-face src url(...)
paths near the top of the <style> block to match your filenames.

which doesn't support ș/ț even in Excalidraw's own release; the app

than showing a broken glyph box.
